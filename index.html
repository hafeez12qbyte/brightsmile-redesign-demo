What Changed

Six changes, each one aimed at more appointments

A redesign is not new paint. Every change below removes a reason a patient would leave and call the next dentist on Google instead.

1 · Booking

BEFORE

"Call during business hours. If you reach our answering machine, we'll call back in 1–2 business days."

AFTER

A 30-second online form — service, preferred day, done. Confirmed by text.

WHY

Most patients search in the evening, when the old site could only offer a closed office.

2 · Mobile experience

BEFORE

A fixed 780-pixel table layout. On a phone: pinch, zoom, squint, give up.

AFTER

Fully responsive with a sticky call/book bar on every phone screen.

WHY

Over 70% of local searches happen on phones — the old site effectively didn't exist for them.

3 · Prices

BEFORE

"Call the office for a complete list of services and current pricing."

AFTER

Starting prices on every service, plus a $89 new-patient special up front.

WHY

Price is the #1 question dental patients have — and the #1 reason they don't call to ask.

4 · Trust signals

BEFORE

A visitor counter, a guestbook, and "best viewed in Internet Explorer 6."

AFTER

A 4.9★ rating, patient reviews, insurance logos, and the dentist's credentials.

WHY

Patients choose healthcare on trust. The old site's signals said "abandoned"; the new ones say "booked solid."

5 · Content & clarity

BEFORE

One giant wall of text, a scrolling marquee, and announcements from a vacation in July.

AFTER

Scannable sections: services, prices, team, reviews, hours — each one click or one scroll away.

WHY

Visitors decide in seconds. Structure is what lets them find "emergency care" while their tooth hurts.

6 · Speed & search

BEFORE

HTML 4 tables, no mobile support, no page descriptions — invisible to modern Google.

AFTER

Clean semantic HTML5, proper titles and descriptions, fast-loading single page.

WHY

Google ranks mobile-friendly, fast pages higher — the redesign is built to be found, not just visited.
