Welcome to the online home of BrightSmile Dental Care, the office of Dr. Robert Keller, DDS, located in Dallas, Texas. We are a full service dental practice providing quality dental care to patients of all ages in the Dallas / Fort Worth metroplex area. Please take a moment to browse our website to learn more about our practice, our services and our staff. If you have any questions please do not hesitate to contact us by telephone during normal business hours. Thank you for visiting and please sign our guestbook before you leave!!

Our services include but are not limited to: general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, teeth whitening, crowns and bridges, dentures, root canals, extractions, dental implants, braces referrals, children's dentistry, gum treatment, fillings, cleanings and much more. Please call the office for a complete list of services and current pricing as prices are subject to change without notice.
